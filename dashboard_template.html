Developer jobs: remote, Nigeria & relocation

How jobs are chosen and labelled

APPLY: strong match with your stack and seniority, and the location is open to you. REVIEW: worth a look, but something needs checking (the remote restriction isn't stated, lead/staff level, a different main stack, a weaker match). Jobs restricted to other countries, onsite abroad without sponsorship, or below US$1,000/month are left out.

Remote – worldwide: the board or advert says anywhere / worldwide. Remote – Africa/EMEA/time zone: the allowed region includes Nigeria, or a time-zone window covers Lagos (UTC+1). Nigeria: based in Nigeria. Visa / relocation: abroad, and the advert mentions visa sponsorship or relocation support. Unclear: remote, but no restriction stated, or the board and advert disagree.

Boards often tag a role "Worldwide" while the advert limits it to certain countries. Where the advert says so, the job is left out or marked Unclear with the advert's words quoted. Always confirm on the advert.
